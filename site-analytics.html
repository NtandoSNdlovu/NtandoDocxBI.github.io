Site Analytics

A personal analytics project exploring how people move through a website and what their behaviour quietly reveals.

At face value, this is a simple dashboard. Underneath, it’s a live data pipeline capturing real interactions in real time. The goal isn’t traffic, it’s intent.

Much like observing movement in a physical space, how visitors navigate, pause, and leave often tells you more than what they say.
