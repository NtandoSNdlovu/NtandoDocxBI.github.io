Extended Professional Experience 💼

Some projects were technically complex, others messy or unclear, and some simply needed to work better for the people using them. These are the challenges I enjoy, and each engagement has refined how I approach the next.

In addition to technical delivery, I focus on clear documentation and written explanations that help stakeholders understand not just what the data shows, but why it matters and how it supports better decisions.

In addition to the fuel stock automation work, I designed a cash variance and control system to improve day-to-day financial visibility at site level.

Daily reconciliation data existed across multiple Excel sheets with inconsistent formats. I built a Power Query–driven consolidation model to standardise this data into a single, reliable structure.

This enabled full cash tracking from till sales through pay-ins, payouts, safes, banking, and expected balances, with early identification of variances.

Dashboards highlighted exceptions beyond agreed thresholds and revealed recurring patterns, giving operations teams the clarity needed to act. The system continues to evolve into an ongoing monitoring tool.

Additional professional engagements are outlined below.

This was one of the most challenging data environments I’ve worked in. Operational data was fragmented, inconsistent, and often incomplete, making visibility and trust difficult.

A key requirement was tonnage integrity, material recorded entering warehouses needed to match what exited. I improved ground-level Excel capture templates by strengthening validations, standardising inputs, and redesigning their structure.

I built warehouse stock reports that provided traders with historical visibility into stock movements, helping surface discrepancies early and verify alignment between reported and physical stock.

Alongside this, I developed an end-to-end mine lot tracking view, following material from mine through warehouses to port and customer, enabling earlier intervention and more informed delivery decisions.

At Mediscor, my work focuses on analytics supporting clinical, operational, and benefit-management decision-making.

A key engagement involved a diabetes management initiative with HaloCare, assessing how proposed rule changes would affect member risk profiles. I built a complex Power Query model to apply and compare new and existing rules.

Results were presented through dynamic tables and visual comparisons, allowing clinical teams to explore the impact interactively before implementation.

I also developed an operational performance dashboard in Power BI, replacing static text-based reports with trend-driven insights around timeouts, failure rates, and scheme performance.

More recently, I’ve contributed to international scheme benchmarking analytics. Due to confidentiality, further details cannot be shared.

LOVOLUXE was an early-stage brand with a small team and limited budget, requiring data to be used practically from day one. This was also where I developed my foundations in analytics.

I analysed competitor pricing, sales trends, and product performance to identify which items sold frequently versus those that delivered meaningful profit.

The analysis supported a shift away from high-volume, low-margin items toward steadier medium-margin products better suited to a lean, online-first operation.

With minimal tooling, I built simple models to compare pricing, quantities, and profitability over time, supporting smarter purchasing and cost control.

This engagement marked the start of my data journey and shaped how I approach data today; focused on clarity, efficiency, and impact.
