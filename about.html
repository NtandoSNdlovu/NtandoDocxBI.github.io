About Me

Data Analyst & BI Specialist focused on building systems that help businesses see clearly and act confidently.

I build like an engineer, think like an analyst, and approach problems like a business partner. That balance shapes how I work, from how data is captured, to how it flows through systems, to how insights are finally presented to decision-makers.

I aim for simplicity because it forces better decisions. When something is clear, it’s usually because unnecessary complexity has been stripped away. Every dashboard, model, or workflow is built with a purpose. If it doesn’t help answer a real question, it doesn’t make the cut.

How I approach data

My first priority is always understanding the data itself, where it comes from, how it’s produced, and what assumptions sit behind it. Only then do I think about visuals, automation, or reporting.

I design with the end user in mind. Executives and analysts consume information differently, and good systems respect that. I enjoy building solutions that serve both audiences without overwhelming one or oversimplifying the other.

This approach extends beyond dashboards. I’ve built end-to-end systems using tools like Excel that behave more like applications, handling validations, approvals, forecasting, and automation.

Efficiency, automation, and AI

I’m comfortable working in environments where data is messy, unstructured, or manual. My focus is building repeatable, efficient processes that reduce effort and improve reliability.

I see AI as an acceleration tool, not a replacement for judgment. Strategy, framing the right questions, and understanding business impact remain human responsibilities.

As my work increasingly involves end-to-end solutions, I’ve been leaning more into data engineering concepts, designing systems that scale while remaining transparent.

Where this started

My interest in data began with a simple question: how can the same information give one organisation an advantage over another?

That curiosity led me to care about the entire process behind insights: how data is captured, how it moves, and how sustainable decisions are made from it.
